§ 02 The Pedagogy

Four days of verbal reps.
Zero silent lectures.

We treat spoken fluency like athletic muscle memory: forty-five minutes of spontaneous, unscripted speaking sparring every single evening.

Experience a demo circle
01

Day 1: 1-on-1 Friction Diagnosis Audio Audit

Your mentor maps your exact hesitation triggers: regional translation lag, pitch dropping at sentence ends, or reliance on fillers like "umm", "basically", and "like".

02

Mon–Thu: Daily Sparring Circles 6 Learners Max

Small-group rooms. Zero teacher monologues. You receive unexpected prompts: rapid rebuttal debates, explaining complex tech to laypeople, and spontaneous crisis defense.

03

Friday: Real Corporate Mock Panels Hiring Leads

High-pressure simulations supervised by real corporate recruitment managers from Deloitte, TCS, and Amazon. You practice situational interview sparring under genuine stress.

04

Sunday: Vocal Diagnostic Scorecard Metrics Telemetry

A quantitative audit of your recorded week: Words Per Minute (WPM), hesitation pause duration, vocabulary diversity, and executive presence index. Concrete numbers, not vague compliments.
